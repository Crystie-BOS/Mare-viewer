# MARE Viewer — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2026**

---

## Summary

MARE Viewer does not independently collect, store, or transmit any user data beyond what is inherent in connecting to Second Life. We do not add analytics, telemetry, tracking, or any data collection of our own.

---

## What MARE Viewer Does Not Collect

MARE Viewer does **not**:

- Collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information
- Add any analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking beyond what is present in the base Kokua viewer
- Log, record, or transmit your login credentials to any server other than Linden Lab's official authentication servers
- Share any data with third parties

---

## Login Credentials

Your Second Life username and password are transmitted directly to Linden Lab's authentication servers over an encrypted connection, exactly as they would be with any standard Second Life viewer. MARE Viewer does not store, copy, or forward your credentials anywhere else.

---

## Linden Lab's Data Practices

When you use MARE Viewer to connect to Second Life, you are connecting to Linden Lab's servers. Linden Lab collects data about your Second Life session as described in their own privacy policy.

For information about what Linden Lab collects and how they use it, please review:

**Linden Lab Privacy Policy:** https://www.lindenlab.com/privacy

---

## Third-Party Components

MARE Viewer is built on the Kokua viewer, which is built on the Linden Lab open source viewer. It includes third-party libraries (such as the Vivox voice library) that may have their own data practices. These are the same components present in the standard Linden Lab viewer. MARE Viewer does not add any new third-party data-collecting components.
