# MARE Viewer — Feature Disclosures

**Please read this document before installing MARE Viewer.**

---

## This is Not a Standard Viewer

MARE Viewer intentionally limits certain features that are present in the standard Second Life viewer and in most other third-party viewers. These limitations are permanent and by design — they cannot be turned off by the user.

This viewer is intended for a specific use case: adult consensual RLV (Restrained Love Viewer) roleplay in Second Life, specifically within the mare and pony roleplay community. If you are not part of this community or do not understand and consent to these restrictions, **do not install this viewer**.

---

## Complete List of Intentional Limitations

The following features are deliberately restricted or removed in MARE Viewer:

1. **Friends list is hidden.** The friends/contacts list is not accessible. Contacts appear as offline/unavailable regardless of their actual status.

2. **Friend online/offline notifications are suppressed.** You will not receive popup notifications when friends log in or log out.

3. **Inbound IM popup notifications are suppressed when RLV IM blocking is active.** Incoming instant messages may be blocked from displaying depending on your RLV collar's configuration.

4. **Group messages are suppressed when RLV IM blocking is active.** Group chat messages may be blocked from displaying depending on your RLV collar's configuration.

5. **The environment/sky setting is locked when your collar commands it.** When your RLV collar sends `@setenv=n`, you cannot set a personal environment override and the viewer uses the sim's environment or shared environment. Released when the collar sends `@setenv=y`.

6. **RLV is always active and cannot be disabled.** MARE Viewer is a Full-Time RLV Viewer (FTRLV). The RLV system is permanently enabled. You cannot turn it off from within the viewer.

## Uninstallation

Installing MARE Viewer does **not** interfere with any other Second Life viewer you have installed. Each viewer uses its own separate installation folder and its own separate settings directory.

To uninstall MARE Viewer:
- Use the Windows uninstaller (Add/Remove Programs / Apps & features), or
- Manually delete the MARE Viewer installation folder

After uninstalling, your other viewers (official LL viewer, Firestorm, etc.) will continue to work normally. MARE Viewer does not modify any shared system settings or registry entries that would affect other viewers.
