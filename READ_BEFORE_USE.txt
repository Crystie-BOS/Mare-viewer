FEATURE DIFFERENCES FROM STANDARD VIEWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following limitations are INTENTIONAL and PERMANENT. They cannot be
turned off by the user:

  1. Friends list is hidden. Contacts appear as offline/unavailable.

  2. Friend online/offline notifications are suppressed.

  3. Inbound IM popups are suppressed when RLV IM blocking is active.

  4. Group messages are suppressed when RLV IM blocking is active.

  5. Environment/sky is locked to sim or shared environment. You cannot
     set a personal environment override.

  6. RLV is always active and cannot be disabled. This is a Full-Time
     RLV Viewer (FTRLV).

If you do not understand or consent to these restrictions, do not use
this viewer.

UNINSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MARE Viewer does not have an installer and does not modify your system.
To remove it, simply delete this folder. It will not affect any other
Second Life viewer you have installed.
